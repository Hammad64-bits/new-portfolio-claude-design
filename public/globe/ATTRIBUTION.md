# Globe data and imagery

- Earth texture: NASA Blue Marble Next Generation, January global composite (`world.200401.3x5400x2700.jpg`), resized locally to 2048 × 1024 for web delivery. NASA Earth Observatory describes the Blue Marble base map and makes the imagery available for public use: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-map/
- Pakistan boundary: Natural Earth 1:50m Admin 0 Countries, filtered to Pakistan from the public-domain Natural Earth vector dataset: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/ and https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_50m_admin_0_countries.geojson

Natural Earth describes country borders as de facto boundaries. The outline is for visual reference, not a legal boundary depiction.
